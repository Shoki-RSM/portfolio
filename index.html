02 Projects

Featured · Team project · Hackathon 2026

ThutoVest

From "I want to study" to "I can graduate."

Built by a team of three for the FNB App of the Year Hackathon 2026 (challenge: Access to Higher Education). ThutoVest walks a matriculant from choosing a course to starting first year. It builds a personal plan, warns them before a deadline, document or funding gap becomes a problem, and connects them with verified sponsors who pay the institution directly. Students low on data can follow along on WhatsApp.

  • Server-enforced rules: the browser is never trusted. Every action is checked by a Supabase Edge Function with Postgres row-level security.
  • Fair funding: bursary limits and funding-gap caps, and money only ever goes to the institution.
  • Child safety (POPIA): under-18s stay hidden from sponsors until a guardian consents and co-signs.
  • Explainable "assistant": deadline and cash-flow forecasts, sponsor matching and scam detection, each with a "Why?" panel.
  • Tested: 99 offline checks plus 38 live attack probes against the deployed backend.
