Projects

Team project · FNB App of the Year Hackathon 2026

ThutoVest

From "I want to study" to "I can graduate."

Built by a team of three for the Access to Higher Education challenge. ThutoVest builds a matriculant a personal plan from course choice to first year. It warns them before a deadline, document or funding gap becomes a problem, and connects them with verified sponsors who pay the institution directly. Students low on data can follow along on WhatsApp.

Live demo ↗ Source code ↗
  • Fair funding rules on the server. Bursary limits and funding-gap caps are enforced there, and money only ever goes to the institution.
  • Child safety under POPIA. Under-18s stay hidden from sponsors until a guardian consents and co-signs.
  • Explainable assistant. Deadline and cash-flow forecasts, sponsor matching and scam detection, each with a "Why?" panel.
  • Tested hard. 99 offline checks, plus 38 attack probes against the live backend.
