02 Projects

Featured · Team project · Hackathon 2026

ThutoVest

From "I want to study" to "I can graduate."

Built by a team of three for the FNB App of the Year Hackathon 2026 (challenge: Access to Higher Education). ThutoVest walks a matriculant from choosing a course to starting first year and connects them with verified sponsors who pay the institution directly.

  • Server-enforced rules: the browser is never trusted. Every action is checked by a Supabase Edge Function and Postgres row-level security.
  • Fair funding: bursary limits and funding-gap caps, and money only ever goes to the institution.
  • Child safety (POPIA): under-18s stay hidden from sponsors until a guardian consents.
  • Tested: 99 offline checks plus 38 live attack probes.
  • React 19
  • TypeScript
  • Tailwind CSS
  • Supabase
  • PostgreSQL
  • Twilio WhatsApp
